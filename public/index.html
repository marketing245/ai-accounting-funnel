The curriculum · 5 modules

The workflows you run every month — rebuilt around AI.

No generic prompt tips. Every module is a real accounting workflow, taught end to end, with the exact way to run it using AI.

M01

Claude 101 — Foundation

Before we touch any workflow, you need to understand how to actually talk to AI — because it's nothing like a Google search. This module covers how to frame your work for Claude, what to ask, how to check what comes back, and why that skill is the foundation everything else is built on.

Result: You go from "I don't know what to ask" to giving Claude work and getting usable output back — in your first session.

Foundation
M02

Journal entries

You take an invoice, a bank statement, a vendor bill — and Claude reads it and gives you the journal entry, correctly classified, with narration. You review, approve, post.

Result: 20 minutes per entry → 2 minutes. You review instead of type.

Ledger
M03

Bank reconciliation & fixed asset depreciation

Paste your bank data and let Claude do the matching. You spend your time on the items that actually don't reconcile — not the ones that do. Depreciation schedules work the same way.

Result: Hours of matching → minutes. Depreciation schedules you can hand to anyone, generated in one pass.

Ledger
M04

GST reconciliation

Claude runs the 2A vs 2B comparison, flags the mismatches, and gives you a clean exception list. You know exactly what needs attention and what doesn't.

Result: A full day of GST matching → one hour of focused review.

Compliance

Month-end close

GL scrutiny, tie-outs, flux notes — this module brings everything together into a close that finishes on time. The capstone where hours saved across every module compound.
