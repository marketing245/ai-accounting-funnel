Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit aiaccountingengineer.com or enroll in the AI in Accounting course, how we use it, and the choices you have. The course is operated by [Legal entity name] ("we", "us"), [Registered address, city, state, PIN].

What we collect

  • Enrollment details — your name, email address and mobile number, entered at checkout. Your email is your student login.
  • Payment data — payments are processed by Razorpay Software Pvt. Ltd. We receive a payment ID, order ID, amount and status. We never receive or store your card number, UPI PIN or bank credentials. Razorpay's privacy policy applies to the payment itself.
  • Learning data — which modules you have marked complete, so your progress is saved across devices.
  • Usage data — pages visited, device and browser type, approximate location and referring site, collected through Google Analytics 4 (with IP anonymisation) and, when advertising is running, Google Ads and Meta conversion tags.
  • Support messages — anything you send us by email or WhatsApp.

How we use it

  • To deliver the course, keep your account secure and save your progress.
  • To send transactional messages: payment confirmation, login help, important course updates.
  • To issue GST invoices and keep the accounting records the law requires.
  • To understand which pages and campaigns work, and to measure advertising results.
  • With your consent, to send you occasional updates about new modules or programmes. You can opt out any time.

Who we share it with

Only service providers who need it to run the course: Razorpay (payments), Vercel (hosting and secure storage), Bunny.net (video streaming), Google (analytics and advertising measurement) and, if enabled, Meta (advertising measurement). We do not sell or rent personal data.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep you logged in to the student portal, and analytics/advertising cookies set by Google and Meta tags. You can block non-essential cookies in your browser; the portal login cookie is required for the portal to work.

Retention and security

Account and purchase records are kept for as long as you have access to the course, and thereafter as required by Indian tax law (currently eight years for financial records). Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Data is stored in private, access-controlled storage and transmitted over HTTPS.

Your rights

Email hello@aiaccountingengineer.com from your registered address. We respond within 7 working days. Deleting your account ends your course access.

Children

The course is intended for working professionals and students aged 18 and above.
